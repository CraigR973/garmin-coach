asks why. It leads with his own numbers.

- **Green:** "Your numbers are all within your usual range."
- **One thing a little off (Green, targets held):** "One thing is a little off: your HRV
  has been below your usual this week (43 ms against 47)."
- **Two things a little off (Amber):** "Two things are a little off: your HRV this week,
  and yesterday was a hard day."
- **One thing clearly off (Amber):** "One thing is clearly off: a very poor night's sleep
  (score 53)."

  (score 58), and 43 hours of recovery still to go."

The phrases it builds from, one per signal:

These lines go to the brief. They also show on Home under "Coach's suggested change"
when a changed session is waiting for his approval.

- **One thing a little off, hard session:** "Move the VO₂ session to a better day this
  week if there is one; otherwise ride it and hold the targets." The week-swap card
  offers the move, as it does today.
- **One thing a little off:** "Hold the prescribed targets rather than pushing past the
  top of each zone." *(Batch 269's line, already signed off)*
- **Amber, hard session:** "Ease the hard intervals a zone; the rest of the session stays
  as planned." The approve card reads: "Ease the hard intervals to ~94% FTP — full
  length."
- **Amber, Zone 2 ride:** "Keep your Zone 2 ride at full length."
- **A planned recovery week** (recovery, taper, consolidation): "This is a planned
  recovery week, so today's session stays as planned: hold the targets." Mark's first two
  weeks back are consolidation and taper, so he will see this one.
- **Red:** unchanged. A Zone 2 ride stays Zone 2, shorter; anything harder becomes an easy
  recovery spin; no VO₂.
