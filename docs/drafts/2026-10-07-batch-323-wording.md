## 2. "Why this plan" (at the top of the plan in the builder)

The lines are built from his data; for Mark they read:

- It keeps your week from Plan No. 2: dumbbells on Monday, VO₂ on Tuesday, Zone 2 on
  Wednesday, sweet spot on Thursday, Friday off, Zone 2 with sprints and dumbbells on
  Saturday, and the long ride on Sunday.
- Week 1 opens with an FTP ramp test on Tuesday 20 October. Ride it in ERG until you can't
  hold the step. Your new FTP is three-quarters of the best minute you held: set it in
  Zwift, and every session follows, because each target is a percentage of FTP.
- Each build week asks a little more than the one before: sweet spot from 2 × 25 minutes to
  2 × 40, VO₂ from 30/30s to 30/15s, and the long ride from 2 hours to 2 hours 15.
- Two dumbbell sessions a week, on Monday and Saturday, legs as well as arms now, getting
  heavier through the plan. Recovery weeks go lighter.
- No week is longer than the longest week of Plan No. 2 (7 h 33).
- Christmas Day and New Year's Day fall on your Friday rest days.
- Nothing changes until you accept it, and you can change any day first.

## 4. Session titles and targets (new; the rest keep Plan No. 2's)

| Session | Title | Target |
|---|---|---|
| The test | FTP Ramp Test | Ramp test: one-minute steps from 50% of FTP, up 6% a minute, in ERG until you can't hold the step |
| VO₂, weeks 2–5 | VO₂ (30/30s, 2 × 10 @ 130%) *(sets and reps by week)* | 130% FTP in ERG, 30s on / 30s easy |
| VO₂, weeks 7–11 | VO₂ (30/15s, 2 × 16 @ 125%) | 125% FTP in ERG, 30s on / 15s easy |
| Strength, Monday | Dumbbells A (legs and back) | Dumbbells 3 × 12 *(by week)* |
| Strength, Saturday | Dumbbells B (upper body) | Dumbbells 3 × 12 *(by week)* |

Strength A: goblet squat, Romanian deadlift, reverse lunge (each leg), one-arm row (each
arm), calf raise, side plank (30 s each side). Strength B: shoulder press, reverse-grip row,
floor press, pullover, curl, triceps extension, step-up (each leg), dead bug. Each says its
sets and reps, how hard ("finish each set with 2–3 reps in reserve"), and to rest about a
minute between sets.

## Decided under Craig's delegation, 6 Oct

1. **The progressions** are the ledger's table, built on Plan No. 2's week and his longest week.
2. **A ramp test, not a 20-minute test,** because it runs in ERG all the way; a 20-minute test
   needs the trainer out of ERG, which his profile rules out.
3. **The card shows from his taper week and after his plan ends,** while a plan is waiting.
4. **A decline sets the plan aside and writes nothing;** he can make a new one in the builder.
5. **His FTP stays 280 W in the plan,** with the test setting the next: the drift-based
   proposal the old builder used is no longer applied.
