## 3. When a change can't be made

| Why | Words |
|---|---|
| Couldn't be read | That change couldn't be read. Try it again. |
| The session has gone | That session isn't in the plan any more. |
| A day outside the plan | That day isn't in the plan: it runs from Mon 19 Oct to Sun 17 Jan. |
| A second ride on a day | Sat 31 Oct already has a ride, and Zwift takes one a day: move or remove that one first. |
| A start that isn't allowed | The plan can start on any Monday from Monday 12 October to Monday 7 December. |
| Days that don't make a week | Each ride needs a day of its own, your rest day has nothing on it, and the two dumbbell sessions go on different days. |
| The ramp test | The ramp test stays as it is. You can move it or remove it. |
| Intervals on dumbbells | Only a ride has intervals to change. |
| A ride with no interval set | This ride has no interval set to change. |
| Minutes on an interval ride | This ride's length comes from its intervals: change those instead. |
| Intervals out of bounds | Reps can be 1 to 20, efforts 10 to 7200 seconds, recoveries 0 to 3600 seconds, and both percentages 40 to 200% of FTP. *(the interval editor's own bounds)* |
| Minutes out of range | A ride can be 20 to 240 minutes, and dumbbells 10 to 60. |
| Too short for its ramps | This ride needs at least 30 minutes for its warm-up and cool-down. |
| An added long ride too short | A long ride can be 60 to 240 minutes. |
| A name too long | A name can be up to 80 characters. |
| Not a ride Zwift can run | That would make a ride Zwift can't run: it needs its warm-up and cool-down. |
| No change | That's how the plan already is. |
| Back to the plan, already there | This is already the plan as proposed. |
| No plan waiting | There's no plan waiting to change. |
| Already accepted | This plan is already in your plan, so it can't be changed here. |
| The page was out of date | The plan has changed since this was shown. Here it is as it stands now. |

The coach describes what it is putting in front of him and never says it has changed, applied,
moved or scheduled anything; one change per answer.

## Decided under Craig's delegation, 6 Oct (Decision #389)

1. **Changing his days rebuilds the plan from the table,** and his changes to single sessions
   go; the builder tells him how many before he confirms, and the log says so after.
2. **The start is any Monday from the next one to eight weeks on,** and his changes move with it.
3. **A changed ride's minutes are its steps rounded up,** as the session interval editor's are.
4. **A week longer than his longest warns and never blocks.**
5. **One ride a day.** A ride can't be moved or added to a day that already has one (dumbbells
   can); the builder doesn't offer those days, and the coach is told the same.
6. **A coach offer applies only at the revision it was made against;** otherwise it is refused in
   the words above and he asks again.
7. **"Why this plan" explains the plan as proposed;** once he has changed it, its heading says so,
   and "Your changes" lists what changed.
