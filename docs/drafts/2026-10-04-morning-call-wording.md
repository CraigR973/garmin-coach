# Today's call in a coach's words: the morning wording

**Status: signed off by Craig on Mark's behalf, 4 Oct 2026, as drafted** (no message to Mark),
with three decisions: the words below; blue for recovery and rest days and red only for health
warnings; and the rest-day rule (a day with nothing planned inside a week the plan covers is a
rest day). To be built as Batch 313; not started, since Craig asked for it to be written up, not
built yet.
Covers every Mark-facing line that names the morning's colour.

## The idea

Two things were fused into one colour word: how recovered Mark is, and what to do today.
They separate:

- **The headline says what to do today.** It comes from the decisions the engine already
  makes for each session (as planned, hold the targets, ease the hard work, easy Zone 2 or
  tempo, recovery spin, shorter Zone 2, off the bike, no training), not from the colour.
- **A small chip says how recovered he is:** Recovered, Some fatigue, Still recovering. It is
  the engine's Green, Amber and Red, in words.
- **Rest days lead with "Rest day"** whatever the reading, and a low reading makes the rest
  "well timed", not a warning.
- **Red is kept for health warnings only.** Recovery and rest days are a calm blue: in Zwift's
  zone colours, which he rides by, blue is easy and red is all-out.
- "Verdict" leaves everything he reads; the app already says "Today's call" in the push and
  the "I disagree" form.

Inside the app nothing changes: the stored colour, the engine, the ride changes, and the rule
that a Red day never sends VO2 to Zwift all stay as they are.

## How a top coach talks, and how the words follow it

1. Says what to do, not what grade you got.
2. Gives the reason in one breath, in body terms.
3. Treats rest and easy days as part of the training.
4. Praises restraint, never scolds; offers a choice where there is one.
5. Saves alarm for real health warnings.

## Training days

Home puts "Good morning, Mark." in front of each line, as now.

**1. Recovered, nothing changes**
- Headline: Green light
- Line: You're recovered. Today's session is on as written.
  - With a hard session today, add: Make it count.
  - With only easy riding today, add: Keep the easy riding easy.
- Chip: Recovered · Look: green, tick

**2. Recovered, one reading a touch off (targets held)**
- Headline: Green light — hold the targets
- Line, no hard session: One reading's a touch off. Today's session is on as written; leave a
  little in the tank.
- Line, hard session: One reading's a touch off. If there's a better day this week, move the
  hard session there; if not, ride it and hold the targets.
- Chip: Recovered · Look: green, tick

**3. Light week (W12 consolidation, W13 taper), session held**
- Headline: As planned — hold the targets
- Line: It's your consolidation week, so the session's already light. Ride it as written and
  hold the targets. ("taper" in W13)
- Chip: Some fatigue (or Recovered) · Look: green, tick

**4. Some fatigue, nothing changes (only easy riding or strength today)**
- Headline: As planned
- Line: There's some fatigue about, and today's plan already suits it. Keep it comfortable.
- Chip: Some fatigue · Look: green, tick

**5. Some fatigue, hard session eased**
- Headline: Take the edge off
- Line: There's some fatigue about. Ride the full session with the hard efforts eased a zone;
  easy riding stays as planned.
- Chip: Some fatigue · Look: amber, gauge

**6. Tired morning (sleep or how he feels clearly off), hard session: he picks**
- Headline: Easy or tempo — your call
- Line: Sleep let you down last night, so the hard efforts are off today. Move the session to
  a fresher day, or pick easy Zone 2 or tempo below.
  - When it's how he feels: You're feeling flat this morning, so the hard efforts are off
    today. …
- Chip: Some fatigue · Look: amber, gauge

**7. Tired morning, long ride offered shorter**
- Headline: Long ride — shorter if you like
- Line: Sleep let you down last night. Ride it as planned, or take the shorter version in one
  tap.
- Chip: Some fatigue · Look: amber, gauge

**8. Still recovering, hard session**
- Headline: Recovery day
- Line: Your body's still recovering. The hard session will do more for you on a fresher
  day — today, rest or an easy spin.
- Chip: Still recovering · Look: blue, charging battery

**9. Still recovering, Zone 2 ride**
- Headline: Short and easy
- Line: Your body's still recovering. Keep today's Zone 2 ride, just shorter — easy miles,
  nothing more.
- Chip: Still recovering · Look: blue, charging battery

**10. Easy day after a fever, or while a chest question is unanswered**
- Headline: Easy day
- Line: Today's hard session becomes an easy spin — the note below explains why.
- Chip: as the reading · Look: blue, charging battery

**11. Health warnings: unchanged**
- "No training today" and "Take today off the bike" keep their signed-off words, the red card
  and the stop icon. They are now the only red on Home.

## Rest days

A rest day is a holiday, a day whose sessions are all skipped, or (new) a day with nothing
planned inside a week the plan covers.

**12. Rest day, recovered**
- Headline: Rest day
- Line: You're recovered — enjoy the day off. Rest is where this week's training turns into
  fitness.
- Chip: Recovered · Look: blue, charging battery

**13. Rest day, some fatigue**
- Headline: Rest day
- Line: Good timing — there's some fatigue about, and today lets it clear.
- Chip: Some fatigue · Look: blue, charging battery

**14. Rest day, still recovering**
- Headline: Rest day
- Line: Well timed — your body's still recovering, and rest is exactly what it needs.
- Chip: Still recovering · Look: blue, charging battery

**15. Holiday**
- Headline: Holiday
- Line: Enjoy the break. The plan picks up when you're home.
- Chip: as the reading · Look: blue, charging battery

**16. No plan loaded for the day (new; Plan No. 2 ends Sun 18 Oct)**
- Headline: No session planned
- Line: There's nothing in your plan for today yet.
- Chip: as the reading · Look: grey

**17. Before the morning is read (pending)**
- Headline: Not ready yet
- Line: Today's call lands once your overnight data has synced.
- Chip: Waiting for data · Look: grey, as now

## Everywhere else Mark reads it

- **Written brief:** the "Today's verdict" section becomes "Today's call". It opens with the
  same headline and chip words as Home, then explains the readings. It never says Green,
  Amber, Red or verdict.
- **Coach chat:** the same words. If Mark says "Red" or "Amber", it answers in kind.
- **Sleep calendar:** each day shows Recovered, Some fatigue or Still recovering (and Rest day),
  with the legend to match, instead of "Green verdict" and G/A/R.
- **Weekly review:** "Verdicts 3G · 2A · 2R" becomes "Mornings: 3 recovered · 2 some fatigue ·
  2 still recovering"; its write-up uses the same words.
- **Moving a VO2 session onto a hard day:** "a Red-readiness day" becomes "a still-recovering
  day".
- **Tomorrow's cue fallback:** "Rest or substitute tomorrow starts from today's recovery
  picture." becomes "Tomorrow's call comes from tomorrow morning's readings."

## What does not change

- The stored colour, the engine, every ride change, and the rule that a Red day never sends
  VO2 to Zwift.
- The health-warning notices and their words.
- The bedroom's Green/Amber/Red room rating (a separate thing).
- The push titles ("Today's brief is ready", "Today's call is ready").
