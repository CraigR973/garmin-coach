Every new line follows something Mark himself reported, names the day he reported it, and
is answered in one tap. The voice is the signed-off states': plain, short, no colour words,
no jargon. The chest-or-heart notice itself is unchanged.

## When it shows

From the morning after a day whose call carried the chest-or-heart warning (his tap, his
note, or "Still there" below), until he says the symptoms have gone **and** that he has
spoken to his GP or 111. It asks every morning until then. A hard session is an easy ride
on those mornings; Zone 2, strength and mobility stay as planned, and the day is never
made "still recovering" for it.

## 1. Home's card

| | Words |
|---|---|
| Heading | Chest or heart symptoms on Tuesday |
| Line, not yet answered today | Have they gone, and have you spoken to your GP or 111? Until you have, hard sessions are easy rides. |
| Line, after "Gone, but I haven't spoken to anyone yet" | Thanks. Hard sessions stay easy rides until you've spoken to your GP or 111. I'll ask again tomorrow. |
| Buttons | Gone, and I've spoken to my GP or 111 · Gone, but I haven't spoken to anyone yet · Still there |
| After a tap | Thanks. Updating today's call. *(existing line)* |

The day is always named ("on Tuesday"), never "yesterday", because the card can show for
several mornings. The buttons stay on the card after "Gone, but I haven't spoken to anyone
yet", so he can say later the same day that he has spoken to someone.

## 2. What each answer does

| Answer | What happens |
|---|---|
| Gone, and I've spoken to my GP or 111 | The card goes. Hard sessions are back from today. |
| Gone, but I haven't spoken to anyone yet | Hard sessions stay easy rides; the card says so and asks again tomorrow. |
| Still there | Today takes the chest-or-heart warning at once, with its notice (unchanged, signed off 2 Oct): no training of any kind today; GP or 111 today; 999 for chest pain or struggling to breathe right now. The card asks again the next morning. |

## Decided under Craig's delegation, 6 Oct

1. **Three answers, one tap each.** Only "Gone, and I've spoken to my GP or 111" ends the
   follow-up, because the guidance (ESC 2020, IOC 2022) is that chest symptoms are assessed
   before vigorous exercise resumes, and the chest notice already tells him so.
2. **Asked every morning until then,** with no time limit (the row's recommendation).
3. **The day is unchanged when no hard session is planned,** as for 303's chest question:
   the alternative made a Zone 2 day read "There's some fatigue about", which is not why.
4. **"Still there" sets the chest-or-heart warning again that day,** under either engine,
   because it is his own report.
