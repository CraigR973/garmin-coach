## Draft

Mark — you were right on all four points today, and on the two from Friday and
Saturday. Here is what has changed and what has not.

**The two Red mornings you said were a Garmin artifact were exactly that, and the
app now spots it by itself.** On 18 and 19 September Garmin lifted your HRV floor
from 45 to 46 for two days and dropped it back. Your own reading never fell — on
the 18th your overnight HRV was 48 ms, your highest in six days, *above* the
raised floor, on your best night of the fortnight. The app now measures the floor
against its own recent history every morning and says so when it has moved under
a reading that held. It also no longer counts those days as strain when it looks
for a pattern, which is what produced the proposal to shift your Tuesday session.
That change went live today.

**The bedroom temperature figures were wrong and are now fixed.** You were right
that it never reached 20 °C. The weekly review was taking the highest reading of
the whole 24 hours, so a warm afternoon in an empty house was being reported to
you as your bedroom overnight. Measured properly, across 7–13 and 14–20 September
your bedroom peaked between 17.9 and 19.7 °C and there were **no** disrupted
nights, not seven and not five. One night genuinely was warm — Sunday the 21st,
at 20.3 °C — and that one still counts. The "average overnight low" figure was
the outdoor weather, and it is now labelled as such.

**Today's contradiction was real and it is not fixed yet.** The brief should not
tell you to stay off the bike and then hand you a ride on the same day. Those are
two different parts of the app that were not speaking to each other.

**The swap was refused for a reason that is our fault, not yours.** Saturday's
session contains six 12-second sprints at 185% of your FTP. The rule that stops a
VO₂ session going out on a Red day looks only at how hard a step is, never at how
long it lasts — so it treats a 12-second sprint as if it were a 2½-minute VO₂
interval and blocked the swap. That is wrong. Twelve-second efforts with three
minutes between them are neuromuscular work, not aerobic work, and your own ride
shows it: 58 minutes, average 176 watts, maximum heart rate 125. Nothing about
that day was a hard aerobic session, and the app should never have refused it.

**One thing to flag, because you will see it before we can fix it.** Because the
swap failed, your plan still shows Tuesday's VO₂ session as completed. It was not
— what you rode was Saturday's Zone 2 with sprints. We could not correct the
record yet, because correcting it runs through the same blocked rule. Both are
being fixed together, and Saturday still has your Zone 2 session on it in the
meantime.

**On today's Red itself, I am not going to tell you it was an artifact.** Your
overnight HRV was 39 ms against a recent 45, the second low night running, and
that is your own reading rather than a moved goalpost. You say you felt fine and
that is worth more than nothing — the ride you did backs you up. But the caution
on the 22nd was not the same error as the 18th and 19th, and it would be
dishonest to lump them together.
