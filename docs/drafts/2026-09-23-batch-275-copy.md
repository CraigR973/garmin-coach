## 1. What 275 does today, in one paragraph

On 19 September Mark said his HRV drops in recovery weeks — three cycles, his own
numbers — and the coach spent three paragraphs declining to call it established.
It was right, and the reason was structural: the only comparator that could have
answered him read the `sleep` table, and HRV lives in `daily_metrics`. The
comparator is now generic over the metric, each metric carries its own threshold
and its own direction, and a hypothesis that binds to no evaluator is refused at
creation instead of stored and left silently unanswerable.

**What it does not do yet:** turn something Mark says in chat into a proposed
experiment (275.4). See §4.

## 2. Decisions that departed from the ledger row — these need Craig

### 2a. 275.5's recommendation cannot be implemented as written

The row recommends **"Mark proposes, Craig confirms"** for who may start an
experiment. That cannot be expressed as a permission check in this system:
**production holds exactly one profile — Mark's — and its role is `admin`.**
Craig has no separate account. An `AdminUser` gate on experiment creation would
be passed by Mark and would restrict nobody.

**What was built instead:** the gate is *bindability*, not identity. `POST
/api/v1/experiments` now refuses any hypothesis that binds to no evaluator, with
a message naming what it would accept. The Experiments page still has no create
form and this batch does not add one, so in practice experiments are still
created deliberately rather than casually.

That achieves what the row was actually worried about — *"an unbounded self-serve
experiment surface invites a dozen underpowered questions and the 'inconclusive,
n too small' answers that started this problem"* — by a mechanism that works in a
single-profile install.

> **Craig: is that acceptable, or do you want a second, non-admin profile so a
> real two-person proposal flow can exist?** The second option is a larger change
> and touches auth, so it is not in this PR either way.

### 2b. One of 275.2's three preconditions was already met

The row says the evaluator has *"a bare difference of means, no variance, no
confidence interval and no significance test"*. **That is no longer true.** Batch
249 (HS240-07) added `mean_difference_interval` — a Welch 95% confidence interval
— and `interval_excludes_zero` gates every directional verdict on it. The
"supported from noise" failure the row describes could not happen today.

The other two preconditions were real and are what this batch adds: a per-metric
threshold and a direction field. A standardised effect size is now reported
alongside the interval as well.

**Nothing in 275.2's intent is weakened by this** — it just means the work was
two thirds rather than three thirds.

## 3. The numbers behind the thresholds — worth a look before you sign anything

Every threshold is **0.3 of that metric's own measured standard deviation**,
because that is what the existing shipped sleep threshold already is (3.0 against
a measured SD of 10.67). Measured over Mark's last 120 days on 23 Sep, one row per
date, morning phase for `daily_metrics`.

| metric | direction | measured SD | threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| age-adjusted sleep score | higher better | 10.67 points | **3.0 points** (unchanged) |
| overnight HRV | higher better | 5.16 ms | **1.5 ms** |
| resting heart rate | **lower better** | 1.47 bpm | **0.5 bpm** |
| time in bed | higher better | 32.21 min | **10 min** |
| REM sleep | higher better | 20.56 min | **6 min** |
| time awake overnight | **lower better** | 17.76 min | **5 min** |

The old flat 3.0 applied to resting heart rate would have been **two whole
standard deviations** — a line nothing could ever cross. Applied to sleep minutes
it would have been under a tenth of one.

## 4. 275.4 is not built, and here is exactly what is missing

275.4 asks that a chat observation become a proposed experiment, reusing the
`conversation_learning_proposals` confirm-before-apply pattern. **It is not in
this PR.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. Its whole surface is Mark-facing copy that has not been reviewed — which is
   why this queue item is PR-only in the first place.
2. It plugs into 275.5's flow, and 275.5's recommendation turned out to be
   unimplementable (§2a). Building the proposal path before that is settled would
   build it against a flow that may not survive.
3. `conversation_learning` is a 985-line Anthropic-backed extractor. A second
   candidate kind, a second destination, an apply path that creates an
   `Experiment`, and a surface is a batch-sized piece of work on its own.

**Recommendation: carry 275.4 forward as its own row** rather than folding it in
here, and specify it once §2a is answered.
