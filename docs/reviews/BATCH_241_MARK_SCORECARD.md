# Your app — a check-up

**1 September 2026**

Every few weeks we go through CheckMark the way you use it — on a phone, first
thing, half awake — and write down what's good, what's annoying, and what we're
going to do about it. This is that write-up, in plain English. There's a longer,
more technical one kept alongside it.

---

## The short version

**The app itself is in the best shape it's ever been.** Almost everything we
flagged in August is now fixed, and we checked it on your own screens rather
than taking anyone's word for it.

**But this morning's brief was missing a lot, and nothing spotted it.** On Sunday
night the app was switched to a newer writing engine. This morning was the first
real brief it produced, and it came out **less than half the length** of
yesterday's — because it quietly dropped several sections rather than because it
had less to say.

That's the one thing on this list worth your attention. The rest is tidying.

---

## What went missing from this morning's brief

Yesterday's brief had eight sections. Today's had four. Gone:

- **Your experiments update.** All four of them — the REM interventions, the
  collagen hold, the 04:00 waking pattern, the recovery-week question. The app
  still ran every one of them this morning, twenty seconds before it wrote your
  brief. It just didn't tell you.
- **The REM pattern section — and its two actions.** *Protect the last
  90 minutes of sleep from early alarms*, and *keep the room cool into the back
  half of the night*. Today's brief still told you your REM was low. It just
  didn't tell you what to do about it.
- **Most of your sleep detail.** You got REM and deep. Light, awake and restless
  went. So did your breathing rate, your oxygen level, your VO₂max, and how much
  your Body Battery charged overnight.
- **The bit where the app owns up.** Yesterday's brief ended by admitting two
  things it had got wrong in earlier reads. That section is gone too — which
  means an app that's making mistakes now looks exactly like one that isn't.

**None of this is your data going wrong.** Everything was collected, everything
was measured, all the analysis ran. It's the writing-up that shrank. We know why
and it's a straightforward fix.

## What it currently gets wrong

**1. If the brief fails and you tap the notification, you get nothing to press.**
The home screen handles a failure well. The brief page — which is where the
"your brief is ready" alert actually takes you — just says "No morning brief
yet, the coach read appears here once today's morning analysis has run." It says
that whether the brief failed, is still being written, or hasn't been started.
There's no Try again on that page. You'd be waiting for something that isn't
coming.

**2. Occasionally an error comes out as computer gibberish.** If the app and the
server ever get slightly out of step, the error message you see is a page of
raw code where your sleep should be. It's meaningless to read and it looks far
more broken than it is.

**3. Your chat history stops about five weeks back.** The coach remembers all
268 messages between you. The app only shows you the most recent 60, and there's
no way to scroll further back. So it remembers more than it lets you see.

**4. Turning notifications back on could fail silently.** They're working fine
right now. But if you ever changed phone, reinstalled the app, or tapped "Don't
Allow" by accident, the button to switch them back on would do nothing and say
nothing. You'd have no way of knowing why the app had gone quiet.

**5. The sleep line on the home screen isn't great.** It reads
"7h 42m asleep · FAIR · REM below your 65–9…". "FAIR" is Garmin's own word,
shouted in capitals, and it disagrees with the brief, which called last night
squarely normal for you. And the useful bit — your personal REM range — gets cut
off mid-number.

**6. Your own words are hidden behind "More".** The free-text box where you
write what actually happened is tucked inside a collapsed section, next to blood
pressure and supplements. It shouldn't be — it's the most useful thing you give
the app. This morning's note about window draughts went straight into the second
paragraph of your brief; your workout note this afternoon set the whole of the
post-workout answer. That field earns its place on the front.

**7. Nothing tells you what the app can't see.** It doesn't know if you're
coming down with something, what medication you're on, or whether something
hurts. On a morning when you're brewing a cold it will still say "Good to go",
because your numbers won't have shifted yet. **If you feel unwell, that beats
the light.** The app should say so on the screen, and at the moment it doesn't.

## What's changing

In rough order of how much difference it'll make to you:

1. **The brief gets its sections back**, and the app gets a check that notices
   if they ever go missing again — rather than relying on someone comparing two
   mornings by hand.
2. **The brief page learns the same manners as the home screen** — if a brief
   failed, it'll say so and give you a Try again.
3. **No more raw error text.** One plain sentence and a button.
4. **A "load earlier messages" button** on the coach chat.
5. **Your notes move to the front** of the check-in.
6. **A permanent line under your verdict** saying what the app can't see, and
   that feeling unwell outranks the green light.
7. Small tidying: the sleep line on the home screen, a couple of fiddly buttons,
   and the timestamp on the brief that currently reads like a receipt.

---

## Nothing you need to do

There's no action for you here. Keep checking in the way you have been — the
notes you write are doing real work, and today's brief quoted both of them back
at you.

One thing worth knowing until the first fix lands: **if a morning brief looks
unusually short, it probably is.** It doesn't mean there was nothing to report.
Ask the coach directly — it still has all of it.
