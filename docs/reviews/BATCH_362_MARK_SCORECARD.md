## The short version

**Overall: B (it was B− on 10 October).** The screens are better than they have ever been, and the way the app reports a problem is now fixed: in eight different breakages we forced, not one showed computer text.

**But the morning of 1 November showed the gap that is left.** Garmin's login stopped working at 00:25. The app had no night of data for Mark until 12:04. What he was told, from about 03:30: "Check in and I'll sync your overnight data", then, after he checked in, "Couldn't finish your brief — Something went wrong while writing today's brief." Nothing said his watch data had not arrived, that it wasn't his fault, or what to do about the ride. The full 120-minute ride sat on Zwift as "Already in Zwift, ready to ride". He started it at 11:59, six minutes before the brief came.

**And one thing he has asked twice with no answer:** the line that says his training readiness has slid. He asked on 28 and 29 October whether the holiday explains it. The brief said the same thing again on 1 November, still with the word "median". He is partly right (it is smaller without the holiday weeks, but still there) and the app never told him.

## What's better since 10 October

- **Computer text is gone.** Eight forced failures, eight plain sentences ("We couldn't load this right now. Your saved data is safe. Try again.").
- **One missing piece no longer blanks the home screen.**
- **The app now says what is on Zwift** when it isn't the planned ride, with a one-tap way back.
- **Home carries a short reason under the call**, in his own units.
- **The FTP card, the memory ask and the next-plan card** are short and in plain words.
- **No contrast failures** in dark mode, none that matter in light.

## What's still wrong

1. **A morning with no data tells him the wrong thing** ("while writing"), nothing about the watch, nothing about the ride.
2. **About six pushes a day**, no way to tune them, and a bedroom alert marked "critical" most evenings from an early reading.
3. **The tired-morning card** points him at a choice it hides, then offers a second button for the same ride.
4. **The readiness line he questioned twice** came back unchanged, with "median".
5. **The brief and the check-in are long**, and REM is told in three different ways.
6. **The call stays on screen after the ride** and still says "pick below".
7. **Small things:** a raw date on the memory page, memory lines written as "Mark prefers…", a statistics table, "Rest is still the plan today" if no plan is loaded.

## What we'd change, in order

1. Say what happened on a no-data morning, and what to do about the ride (with the engine's own caution). **Before Tuesday 10 November.**
2. One bedroom push in the evening, one ride nudge a day, and a switch for each kind.
3. One control for the tired-morning ride, shown on Home straight away, with the default said out loud.
4. Answer the readiness question once, with his point in it, then only when it changes.
5. Shorter check-in (REM questions only on the day they matter) and a shorter brief with one REM range.
6. The small ones, together.

These are recommendations to Craig; he decides what lands and when.
