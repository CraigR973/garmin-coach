## The one worth understanding

Until this week, the app kept **one** record per day, and it got overwritten by
the evening.

Take **Saturday 15 August**. That morning the app told you your readiness was
**69 — moderate**, with essentially no recovery time outstanding. By the evening,
after the day's training, your watch had revised that same day down to **26 — low,
with 47 hours on the recovery clock**. The app kept only the evening version.

So every later question — *was that week hard? what's my normal readiness? did
that day count as a bad one?* — was being answered from a number you were never
shown, on a day the coaching had called moderate.

**That's now fixed.** The app keeps both: the reading you were given at wake, and
the settled reading once the day closed. Each part of the app takes the one it
actually means. We watched the first live run of this happen this morning, and it
did exactly the right thing.
