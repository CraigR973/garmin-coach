## One thing that needs tightening

When you tell the app *why* a morning was bad — a hard block, a late night, a
drink — it now uses that to decide whether the morning counts toward its
longer-term "you may need a deload" signal. The intent is right: a one-off
explained bad night shouldn't be treated as creeping overtraining. But right now
it takes your explanation at face value a bit too readily, and it can set aside a
morning where your body's own numbers were saying something. We're going to make
it check your explanation against your physiology before it discounts a day.

**Something to know right now:** because of that, the seven-day deload the app
proposed on 1 August has been withdrawn from this week's reads — but two of its
sessions (Sat 8th and Sun 9th) are still sitting in your plan as unaccepted
"chronic deload" suggestions. If they look inconsistent with what the app is
telling you this week, that's why, and you can safely ignore them.
