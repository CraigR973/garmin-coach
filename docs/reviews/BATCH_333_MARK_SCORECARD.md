# Your app — a check-up

**10 October 2026**

Every few weeks we go through CheckMark the way you use it — on a phone, first thing, half
awake — and write down what's good, what's annoying, and what we're going to do about it. This
is that write-up, in plain English. The longer, technical one sits alongside it
(`BATCH_333_UX_LIVE_APP_REVIEW.md`). For Craig to read as if through Mark's eyes; not sent to Mark.

---

## The short version

**Screen by screen, the app is better than it has ever been.** Most of what we flagged on
1 September is fixed, and we checked it on your own screens — this morning's included.

**But the app still doesn't tell you which ride is actually waiting in Zwift.** When the coach
changes a ride, or you move one, the screens keep showing the session from your plan and say
"Already in Zwift, ready to ride" — whatever Zwift really holds. That is what happened with the
Sweet Spot on Thursday, and it happened again this morning when you moved it to Sunday.

**Overall: B−.** Up from September on almost every screen; down on the one thing that matters
most on a bike day — knowing what you're about to ride.

---

## The one thing worth knowing before Sunday

On Thursday you approved the coach's easy ride *and* swapped the Sweet Spot to Saturday. The
swap carried the easy ride with it. This morning you moved the "Sweet Spot" on to Sunday, and the
easy ride went too. So on Sunday the app will show a 58-minute Sweet Spot, and Zwift will have a
**29-minute easy spin, nothing above 60 %**, named "Recovery substitution".

Until it's fixed: **the name of the workout in Zwift is the truth.** If it says "Recovery
substitution", that's the easy ride, not the Sweet Spot.

## What's better since 1 September

- **The brief page knows when the write-up failed**, says so, and lets you retry. Your call and
  your plan are there either way.
- **Your chat history is all there**, and the chat opens at the latest message.
- **Your own words show on the home screen** right under the day's call.
- **No colour on the home screen.** The call is in words: "Take the edge off", "Recovery day".
- **The app tells you when Zwift isn't getting rides** — on the home screen — and what to do
  about it, after the intervals.icu account went quiet in September.
- **The next plan asks you first.** From Monday the home screen shows Plan No. 3 with "Review
  it", and nothing changes until you accept.

## What's worse, or still wrong

1. **You can't see what's in Zwift.** Covered above. It's the most important fix on this list.
2. **Two fixes offered at once.** On Thursday the home screen offered "move it to Saturday" and
   "approve the easy ride" together, with nothing saying "pick one". You did both, which is a
   perfectly reasonable reading of the screen.
3. **Offers that don't notice you've already acted.** The "move it" button and the "Sweet Spot
   0/1" count stayed all day after you'd moved it, and the coach's "at risk" message came from
   the same out-of-date picture.
4. **Computer text when something fails.** Flagged in September and still not fixed.
5. **The REM line you couldn't follow** is still there, word for word. Your own version — "if
   I have a bad night, stick to my normal day and bedtime" — is clearer, and should replace it.
6. **The plan builder is a long scroll** and asks for seconds where you think in minutes.
7. **If you decline Plan No. 3**, from 19 October the app will say "Rest is the plan today"
   rather than "nothing is planned" — which reads as if it planned the rest.

## What's changing

In rough order of how much difference it'll make to you:

1. **The ride card will say what's in Zwift** whenever it differs from the plan — and until
   then, the app will stop offering to move a ride that has already been changed.
2. **One choice, not two buttons**, when the coach offers alternatives — and offers disappear
   once you've acted on them.
3. **Plain sentences instead of computer text** when something fails.
4. **"No plan loaded"** when there isn't one, with a link to make the next.
5. **Your wording for the REM line.**
6. **A tidier plan builder**: minutes, the later weeks folded away, and "are you sure?" on Decline.

These are recommendations to Craig; he decides what lands and when (most wait until after
the 21 October trial review).

## Nothing else you need to do

Keep checking in and keep telling the coach when something's off — your corrections on 8 October
were right on both counts. The one habit worth having for now: **glance at the workout name in
Zwift before you start.**
