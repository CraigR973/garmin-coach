## C. The evidence, line by line

**What a daily readiness check should do for Mark.** He is 57, VO2max about 55, FTP 280,
13-week 2121 blocks, five rides a week, goal "meaningful benefits, not obsessive marginal
gains" (knowledge base, handover). The evidence I read supports four jobs:

1. Catch the warnings that matter at his age, whatever the numbers say: chest symptoms,
   palpitations, faintness, fever. AF is about five times commoner in long-term male
   endurance athletes (Abdulla 2009, mean age 51).
2. Time hard sessions onto good days rather than delete them. HRV-guided groups did fewer
   hard sessions (Vesterinen 2016: 13 vs 18) with similar or slightly better gains.
3. Judge HRV on weekly averages against his own normal, not single nights (Plews 2013
   IJSPP; Plews 2014: at least 3–4 readings a week).
4. Count what he feels: self-report tracks training load at least as well as objective
   measures (Saw 2016).

**How noisy his nights are** (*computed*, 99 nights): mean 46.9 ms, SD 4.9 ms (CV 10.4%),
lag-1 correlation 0.21. The 7-day mean's SD is 2.25 ms, about 0.46 of a night's. Garmin's
overnight HRV (the average of 5-minute windows across sleep) differed from ECG by 10.5% on
average, limits −15 to +12 ms, in the one independent validation I found (Dial 2025, Fenix
6, 13 adults). A single night's dip is mostly noise; a week's is not. **None of the trials
used a wrist's overnight average**: they used 90 s to 4 min supine chest-strap readings on
waking.

## Limitations

- **One man, about 100 mornings.** Every rate above is uncertain; the outcome proxies are
  directional.
- **Papers.** I read Javaloyes 2019 and Vesterinen 2016 in full (accepted manuscripts),
  Alfonso 2025 and Dial 2025 in full text, and the abstracts of Plews 2012, 2013 (both
  papers) and 2014, Kiviniemi 2007, Saw 2016, Gabbett 2016, Impellizzeri 2020 and Abdulla
  2009; Plews 2013 Sports Med is paywalled. The neck check and the sleep-and-exercise claims
  are cited as convention, not re-read. Garmin's load-ratio ranges are from its manual.
- **Not verified:** whether Sentry emails Craig; what Zwift shows today; whether Home showed
  an Approve button for the 1 Oct proposal; the 25 Sep quote's source; a live strength read
  under Batch 288.
- **What-ifs are computed, not run:** the persistence rule and the readiness counterfactual
  re-combine the replay's domain ratings outside the engine.
- **No clinician** reviewed the medical findings. This is not medical advice.
