# Your Coach — Honesty & Safety Scorecard

**Refreshed 26 July 2026**

A periodic check that CheckMark is giving you straight, safe guidance — not just
telling you what you want to hear. This is the plain-English version; a more
technical one is kept internally.

## The headline

**Your coach is honest with you, and it held that honesty as we added new
features.** It doesn't hand out easy Greens, it's candid when a night was poor, and
it now understands your indoor ERG setup so it reads your power fairly.

**Overall: strong — B+.**

## In your own recent data (10–26 July)

- **16 mornings graded:** 11 Green, 1 Amber, 4 Red. A meaningful share of the harder
  signals were caught — this is not a rubber-stamp.
- **The four-Red stretch (19–22 July)** came straight from your HRV and sleep
  numbers — the coach did not soften them, even on the morning your sleep score
  itself looked fine.
- **25 July VO₂ (indoor, ERG):** it read your interval power fairly and treated the

## Being straight with you

No check is ever "perfect." The honest caveat: the coach is very good at catching a
*single* bad day, and we are still strengthening how it spots a *slow* build-up of
fatigue over many weeks. That is the next thing on the list to improve — and it is
part of why we run this review.
